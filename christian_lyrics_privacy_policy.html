1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Christian Songs mobile application ("we", "our", or "the Application"). We are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and transparency for our users.

Christian Songs is an offline-capable, ad-free hymn and lyrics viewer application created strictly for devotional and informational purposes.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We do NOT collect, transmit, store on remote servers, sell, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).

  • Personal Data: The application does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location. Any names or notes entered within the app are stored exclusively on your device.
  • Device Permissions: The application requests standard android.permission.INTERNET capability. It does NOT access your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, photos, or hardware identifiers.

3. Zero Advertising & Third-Party Trackers

  • 100% Ad-Free: The application does not contain advertisements, ad SDKs, banner ads, interstitial ads, or rewarded ads.
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: We do not integrate third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK) or tracking services.
  • No Data Sharing: Because no data is collected, no data is sold or shared with any third party.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

The Application is designed for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age, ensuring full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All bookmarks, custom songs, and local preferences reside entirely within the app's local device sandbox. You can erase all data at any time via Device Settings > Apps > Christian Songs > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.
