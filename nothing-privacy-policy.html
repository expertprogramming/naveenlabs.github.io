1. Information Collection and Use

The Nothing application is designed to be a lightweight entertainment utility that cycles phrases and changes background colors. The application:

  • Does not collect any personal identification information (such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address).

2. Permissions

The application requires zero (0) permissions to operate on your Android device. It does not request access to the Internet, Storage/Photos, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, or any other sensitive system services.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, services, or trackers. Specifically:

  • No third-party analytics libraries (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase).
  • No advertisement networks or advertising libraries (e.g., Google AdMob).
  • No social media integrations or external API communication.

Because the application collects no information of any kind, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy frameworks. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children of any age.
