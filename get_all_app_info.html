1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application GetAllInstalledAppInfo (the "Application"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our users. This policy clearly explains how information is handled within the Application.

2. Information Collection and Use

GetAllInstalledAppInfo does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

The Application's sole utility is to query and display the list of launchable applications locally on your Android device. All processing is executed entirely in device memory:

  • No package lists or application data are uploaded or shared.
  • No unique identifiers (such as Android ID, IMEI, or Advertising ID) are accessed, stored, or processed.
  • The Application operates completely offline and does not require or request Internet access (android.permission.INTERNET).

3. Permissions

The Application requests no sensitive, dangerous, or user-facing platform permissions. Specifically:

  • No Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, SMS, or Storage permissions are requested or used.
  • No Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission is requested or used.
  • No broad package querying permissions (such as QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) are used.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Application contains no advertisements and does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), tracking tools, or ad networks (such as Google AdMob). No third parties have access to any data through this Application.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Because no data is collected whatsoever, the Application is fully compliant with COPPA and global child data protection regulations.

6. Security

Because the Application does not collect, process, or transmit any data over the internet, your device information remains strictly confidential and contained solely within your local device operating system.
