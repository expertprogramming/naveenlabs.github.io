# 🔒 IPTools Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** August 27, 2026 **Last Updated:** August 27, 2026 **Developer:** Naveen Labs (`experprogramming.org`) --- ## 1. Introduction & Core Privacy Commitment **IPTools** ("the Application", "we", "our") is designed as a privacy-first, professional network diagnostic and calculation suite. We believe your network telemetry, IP addresses, and hardware details belong exclusively to you. * We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data. * We do **not** store your scan logs, network passwords, or private IP histories on remote central servers. * Diagnostic operations (such as LAN scanning, port scanning, ping, and traceroute) are executed locally on your device or directly against your target servers. --- ## 2. Information Collected & Used ### A. Network & Device Telemetry (Processed Locally) To provide core networking diagnostics, the app reads technical parameters directly from your device's operating system: * **Wi-Fi & Network State**: SSID name, BSSID, Signal Strength (dBm), Link Speed, Subnet Mask, Gateway IP, and Local IP address. * **Hardware & Battery Data**: Device model, CPU architecture, RAM usage, and battery temperature/voltage. * *Usage*: This information is processed on-device in real-time to display diagnostic metrics. It is never uploaded to any remote analytics database. ### B. Location Permissions (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) * **Why Required**: Beginning in Android 8.0 through Android 15+, Google Android OS requires Location access for applications to read the connected Wi-Fi Network Name (SSID) and BSSID. * **Usage**: IPTools uses this permission solely to identify the Wi-Fi network you are connected to. We do **not** track, record, or transmit your physical GPS coordinates. ### C. Public IP & DNS Lookup Requests * **External Diagnostics**: When using tools like Public IP Lookup, GeoIP, DNS Propagation, or Speed Test: - The app queries public diagnostic servers (such as M-Lab NDT7 endpoints, Cloudflare, Google DNS, and IPify). - These requests transmit standard HTTP/WebSocket headers necessary to perform the requested test. --- ## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob) IPTools is a free-to-use application supported by Google AdMob. Third-party advertising partners may collect pseudonymous device identifiers to deliver relevant ads in accordance with Google Play policies: * **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: - May collect Advertising ID (AAID), coarse location, and app interaction data to serve ads. - Privacy Policy: [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) * **Google Play In-App Review & Core Services**: - Used to handle in-app review flows and application updates. --- ## 4. Data Retention & Security * **Local Storage**: Preferences (such as theme selection and speed test unit configurations) are stored locally in your device's sandboxed `SharedPreferences`. * **No Account Required**: IPTools does not require registration, login, or email authentication. --- ## 5.
