Privacy Policy for LocalShare

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 • Application: LocalShare (Android & Desktop) • Developer: Naveen Labs
🔒 Our Core Privacy Commitment

LocalShare is built from the ground up on a zero-knowledge, local-first architecture. Your files, metadata, and personal data never touch external servers, databases, or cloud providers. All transfers occur directly peer-to-peer (P2P) between your authenticated devices on your local network.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

LocalShare operates without accounts, logins, telemetry, or remote servers. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No File Contents or Metadata: The files you transfer (photos, videos, documents, folders) are never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • No Analytics or Covert Telemetry: LocalShare does not include background behavioral analytics or user-profiling trackers (e.g., no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Facebook SDKs). Any advertising delivery is strictly governed as disclosed in Section 7 below.
  • No Location Tracking: We never record, process, or transmit your geographical coordinates (GPS location).

2. How Local Transfers & Encryption Work

All communication in LocalShare occurs exclusively within your local area network (Wi-Fi or Direct Hotspot):

  • Device Discovery: The app uses standard Local Area Network (LAN) UDP multicast/broadcast and TCP pings to find other LocalShare nodes connected to the same Wi-Fi subnet.
  • End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): Transfers are secured with X25519 Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) session key exchange and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Only the intended sender and receiver hold the ephemeral cryptographic keys.
  • Ambient PIN Authentication: To prevent unauthorized incoming connections, device pairing requires matching a 6-digit Short Authentication String (SAS) security code on both screens.

3. Android System Permissions & Usage

LocalShare requests only the minimum runtime permissions required to facilitate local offline file transfers:

Permission Purpose & Scope
Nearby Devices (API 33+)
NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES		 Used to discover nearby LocalShare peers on local Wi-Fi and host offline Wi-Fi Direct hotspots. Configured with usesPermissionFlags="neverForLocation" so physical location is never derived.
Location (Legacy API ≤ 32)
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION		 Only requested on legacy Android 8–12 devices where the Android OS mandates location permission for local Wi-Fi hardware scanning. LocalShare never reads or records GPS coordinates.
Camera (Optional)
CAMERA		 Used exclusively for optical scanning of QR codes (to connect to Web Share portals and Wi-Fi Direct Hotspots). No photos or video frames are ever saved, stored, or transmitted.
Storage & Media
READ_MEDIA_* / SAF		 Used to read the specific files or folders you explicitly choose to send, and to save incoming received files into your device's Download/LocalShare directory.
Notifications
POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 Used to display active file transfer progress, streaming velocity, remaining time (ETA), and incoming transfer approval alerts.
Foreground Service
FOREGROUND_SERVICE		 Ensures uninterrupted file streaming when the app is minimized or the phone screen turns off during large multi-gigabyte transfers.

4. Data Storage and Retention

LocalShare stores minimal operational data strictly on your local device:

  • Transfer History & Vault: A local SQLite database keeps a log of your sent and received files, file sizes, and timestamps. This data remains on your physical storage and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • User Control: You can clear your transfer history or delete received files at any time through the app's Received Vault screen or via your device's file manager. Uninstalling the app removes its private database.

5. Zero-Install Web Share Portal

When you use the "Share via Web Browser" feature, LocalShare spins up an ephemeral HTTP server on your local Wi-Fi IP (port 53317). Only devices connected to your exact same Wi-Fi router who possess the session PIN or scan the QR code can access the shared files. The web portal terminates immediately when closed.

6. Children's Privacy

LocalShare does not collect personal information from any individuals, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To support ongoing development and keep LocalShare free for all users, the application may display advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, such as Google AdMob.

These third-party ad networks may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and device information, including:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or other device identifiers.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Coarse Location: General approximate location derived from network IP address (LocalShare does not share GPS coordinates).

This data is collected directly by Google AdMob in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. For detailed information on how Google manages advertising data, please review:

  • Google Advertising Policies & Technologies
  • Google Privacy Policy

User Control & Opt-Out: You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking at any time through your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
