Privacy Policy

Application: IPTools (Android)  |  Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Privacy-First Standard: IPTools does not collect personal identities, sell user data, or store private network scan logs on any external remote server. All diagnostic analysis runs directly on your device.

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

IPTools ("the Application", "we", "our") is built as a professional, Swiss-army-knife network diagnostic and calculation suite for Android. We respect your digital privacy and ensure that network configurations, Wi-Fi parameters, and device telemetry remain in your full control.

2. Data Processed Locally On-Device

To provide real-time network and hardware diagnostics, the application reads technical OS parameters directly from your device:

  • Network Identity & State: Connected Wi-Fi SSID, BSSID, Signal Strength (dBm), Link Speed, Subnet Mask, Gateway IP, DNS Server addresses, and Local Private IP.
  • Hardware Profile: Device model, processor architecture, RAM utilization, and battery telemetry (temperature, voltage, charge level).

Note: All the above diagnostic telemetry is processed in-memory in real time and is never transmitted to any external analytics server.

3. Android Operating System Permissions

  • Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Android 8.0 through Android 15+ strictly enforces location permission as a requirement for apps to read the connected Wi-Fi Network Name (SSID) and BSSID. IPTools uses this permission exclusively to identify your connected Wi-Fi network. We do NOT track, record, or share your physical GPS coordinates.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to perform network diagnostics such as Speed Test, Ping, DNS Propagation, Public IP lookup, and Traceroute.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

IPTools is a free application supported by non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdMob. Advertising partners may process standard pseudonymous identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID) to deliver relevant ad content and protect against fraudulent traffic:

  • Google Mobile Ads: Subject to Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play In-App Review: Handles in-app feedback dialogs natively through Google Play Services.

5. Data Security & Storage

Your custom app settings (e.g. Dark/Light Theme preferences, Speed Test unit settings) are stored strictly on your local device in sandboxed storage. IPTools does not require user registration, logins, or cloud accounts.
